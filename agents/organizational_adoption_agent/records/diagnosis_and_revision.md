# Weakness preserved and revision
Initial failure mode: an unspecific organizational-readiness checklist can score overall readiness as sufficient because of research culture, executive sponsorship or available capital, even when training, local governance and fallback remain unverified. That would confuse preparedness to *plan* an evaluation with readiness for live-patient procedures.

Revision: explicit clinical-launch veto gates; U for unknown proof; 0–3 dimension ratings with evidence origin; proposed owner + objective closure document for every critical action; no weighted average that masks a safety failure. Added Hospital C stress case to test whether sponsor urgency could override constraints.

Persistent limitation: all hospitals are synthetic, and no competency files, local protocols, operating-cost records or vendor contracts were available. Structural JSON validation is not proof of clinical correctness. A trust-specific result must abstain from go-live until these records are checked.
